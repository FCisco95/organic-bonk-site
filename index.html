What is Organic?

Organic is a decentralized community. No one owns it, anyone can join. We ship memes, tools, and real work together. Our token is the flag, but the product is the people. Builders, artists, growth hackers, translators, space hosts, mods, everyone has a lane. We keep the treasury public, fund tasks, and share revenue from anything we create.

Why are we different?

  • Real people, no paid bots, no fake metrics
  • Public treasury, public votes, public receipts for every payout
  • Roles for all skills, small tasks anyone can grab, bigger sprints we plan as a group
  • Tools and brand in one place, born on Bonk, open to collabs and new characters
  • Revenue goes back to contributors, split by proof of work and community rules
  • If our numbers look lower, it is because they are real, not botted

Our mission

Grow a community that builds for itself and for others. Fund small tasks every week, ship useful tools and good visuals, help good projects get a lift, and split the upside with the people who show up. The path is set by votes. The further we onboard new people, the further we go.
